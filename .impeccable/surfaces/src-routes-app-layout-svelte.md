## Direction contract

Scope: app shell, login, profile and placeholder screens. Mode: Operate. Task: reach and log a movement one-handed on a phone.

THESIS: The shell is the ledger's cover: a calm, bank-grade frame that feels pressed, not printed. It refuses the fintech default of gradients, glass, hype and chart glitter.

OWN-WORLD: Forest Ledger Green on Warm Paper (Signal Lime becomes primary in dark), hairline borders and tonal layering, DM Sans, pill controls, 0.75rem fields and cards, and exactly one lifted element, the center "+" FAB. Recognizable with all content removed by the ledger mark, the pill active tint and the green/lime pairing.

STORY: The owner just spent or received money. They trust the frame at a glance and reach "new movement" with one thumb in a second.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone width. Sticky top bar with the ledger mark and "Gastar" wordmark at left and the profile circle at right. The content column is max 560px. Fixed bottom nav on the surface ground with a hairline top border and five slots, with the lifted "+" in the center, and the active item in a primary-tinted pill.

FORM: Extension of the established world (no concept roll, seed key: none). The shell is built first, then shared components, then login, profile and the placeholder screens.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
